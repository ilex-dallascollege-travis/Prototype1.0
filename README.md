## Taxes & Money Warnings

If you use this bot (or build your own) with real money, taxes are your
responsibility. This is general information, not tax advice. Talk to a
qualified tax professional (CPA) about your situation.

- **Short-term gains are taxed heavily.** Profits on investments held one year
  or less are taxed as ordinary income at your regular rate, which is usually
  higher than the long-term rate. A trading bot will produce mostly short-term
  gains.
- **Self-employment tax is a different tax.** It applies to income from
  self-employment (freelancing, selling services, etc.). Gains from trading
  your own account are generally treated as capital gains, not self-employment
  income, but other income you earn (for example, selling this bot or running
  other people's money) may be subject to it.
- **Set money aside.** Taxes are owed on profits as you earn them, and you may
  need to make estimated quarterly payments to the IRS. Don't spend gains you
  haven't set aside taxes for.
- **Watch out for wash sales.** Re-buying a security shortly after selling it
  at a loss can disallow the loss for tax purposes.
- **Losses have limits.** The amount of net trading losses you can deduct each
  year against other income is capped.
- **Keep records.** Save broker statements, 1099-B forms, and your bot's trade
  logs.
- **Rules change and vary.** This applies to US federal taxes. State taxes and
  rules differ and change over time.
- **Taxes can turn a "profit" into a loss.** A strategy that looks profitable
  before taxes and fees may not be after them.
